The 3 steps, with real screenshots

How to claim your profile, screen by screen

These are screenshots from our registration, in Portuguese, just as they appeared to us.

1

Find your business

Go to chatgpt.com/business-hub (or use the ChatGPT sidebar) and search for your business name. Search the short name and the full name. Found yours? Choose the one with your address. If you can't find it, click Create.

2

Prove the business is yours

First, it asks how you serve customers. Physical location (shop, practice, office): proof is usually a code sent to your business phone. Online store: it asks for your website and a line to add to your site's DNS, the "address book" for your domain. You can find this in the control panel of the company where you bought your domain (Registro.br, Hostinger, GoDaddy, Cloudflare…): send this screenshot to whoever manages your website, and they can do it in a minute, or let the agent handle it. You may also be able to verify through your business email or Shopify store, if ChatGPT offers those options.

3

Tell ChatGPT who you are

Choose a category from a list, write a description of up to 300 characters, and add delivery and returns details. Write the way you want ChatGPT to talk about your business. Then verify your identity with an ID and a selfie: without that, the profile stays a draft. Then invite your team.

How we did it

An agent handled registration on its own

We did not fill out the form by hand. We asked Claude Code, an AI agent, to use our browser, where we were already logged in. It clicked, read the screens, filled everything in, and stopped only at the steps that require the owner.

  1. Opened the Business Hub

  2. Created the profile

  3. Paused and asked for approval

  4. Filled in the rest

  5. Delivered the report
    screenshots of every screen and what is still missing: identity verification, which only the owner can complete.

Tips

What we learned by doing it ourselves

All of this happened during our registration. The full kit includes 19 tips.

🔎 Search both ways

Try the short name and full name. A namesake is the most common problem: "INEMA" brought up another business.

🏢 Use the business account

Whoever claims the profile becomes its owner. Don't use a staff member's personal account if they might leave.

✂️ 300 characters, no warning

The description gets cut off mid-word. Count the characters first. The kit includes a checker.

⏳ The DNS line expires

After about 24 hours. Add it and verify on the same day. And don't delete it afterward.

📋 Categories come from a list

Search by keyword. There is no "course" category for online courses, so we chose "AI Company."

📞 Phone and email are public

They are optional. Only add contact details where customers can reach you.

↩️ Returns in Brazil

Online purchases already come with a 7-day cooling-off period. We selected "free, 7 days."

🧩 Extensions can get in the way

A browser extension opened over the page and hijacked keystrokes. Close your extensions or use a clean window.

🪪 Without identity verification, it is a draft

Your ID and selfie unlock the dashboard, business agent, and full editing access.

Common questions

Before you start

Do I have to pay?

Not if you do it yourself: claiming the profile is free, and we used a free ChatGPT account. Paid offers (ads, Business plan) are available but not required. Only the agent option uses a separate paid service (the Claude plan for Claude Code).

Does it work in Brazil?

It worked for us in Portuguese on 11/22/2026. It is in beta: if it is not available to you yet, the kit will be useful once it is.

Do I need to know how to code?

No. The 3 steps involve clicking and filling in a form. Whoever manages your website can handle the DNS line, or the agent can do it with your approval.

What if someone has already claimed my business?

Sources mention an option to dispute ownership and a support team for locked profiles. We did not run into this; let us know if it happens to you.

Will this put me at the top of the answers?

There is no promise of ranking. The benefit is that ChatGPT has your correct, verified details, and you can see how it talks about you.

Can I do this on my phone?

We did it on a computer. The Business Hub opens in a browser; adding the DNS line and using the agent require a computer. If your case only requires a phone code, the rest is just clicking and filling in a form.

Can the agent make mistakes?

Yes, which is why it pauses before each sensitive step and shows you what it is going to do. You review the description before it submits.
